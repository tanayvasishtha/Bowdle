Bowdle privacy notice

Last updated 16 September 2026

Bowdle is a browser game run by Tanay Vasishtha. This page lists what the game stores about you, why, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Account: a random account id, the explorer name you choose, your XP, Ink, equipped cosmetics and the cosmetics you own.
  • Sign-in token: a random secret kept in your browser's local storage. The server keeps only a one-way hash of it.
  • Season stats: kills, wins and matches played in each season, shown on the public leaderboard next to your explorer name.
  • Match rewards: which match ids have already paid out to your account, so rewards are never granted twice.
  • Linked sign-in (optional): if you choose Discord or Google, we store that service's account id for you and read your display name once. We do not receive your email address or password.
  • Purchases (web version only): an order id, the item bought and its status. Payment details are handled by Xsolla and never reach our servers.
  • Settings: key bindings, volume and similar preferences stay in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No chat, no messaging and no voice.
  • No advertising cookies or trackers set by Bowdle.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for advertising.

Services involved

  • Hosting: the game server and database run on a cloud host, which processes your IP address to deliver the game.
  • Google Fonts: the game's fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address when they load.
  • Discord and Google: only if you choose to link them.
  • Xsolla: processes payments on the web version under its own privacy policy.
  • Game portals: when you play on Poki or CrazyGames, the portal runs its own SDK and ads under its own privacy policy. Portal versions have no purchases and no linked sign-in.

Game clips

The Save clip button records the game view in your browser and saves the file to your device. Clips are never uploaded by Bowdle.

How long we keep data

Account data stays until you delete the account. Order records may be kept longer where tax or payment rules require it.

Deleting your data

Open Profile in the main menu and choose Delete account. This removes your account, tokens, stats, rewards history and owned cosmetics right away. Clearing your browser storage also removes the local token and settings.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/tanayvasishtha/Bowdle.
