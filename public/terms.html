Bowdle terms of play

Last updated 16 September 2026

These terms cover playing Bowdle, a browser game run by Tanay Vasishtha. By playing you agree to them.

Playing fairly

  • No cheats, bots, modified clients or exploits. The server decides every hit.
  • Explorer names must not be offensive or impersonate someone. We may rename or remove accounts that break this.
  • Do not attack, overload or scrape the game servers.

Ink and cosmetics

  • Ink is earned by playing. It cannot be bought, sold, traded or exchanged for money.
  • Cosmetics change how things look only. They never change damage, speed, hitboxes or cooldowns.
  • Every price is fixed and shown before you buy. There are no random paid rewards.
  • Items are a licence to use them in Bowdle. They cannot be traded, gifted, resold or cashed out, and have no money value.

Purchases (web version)

  • Payments are processed by Xsolla, which acts as the seller of record and shows the final price, taxes and refund terms before payment.
  • A purchase needs a linked Discord or Google sign-in so the item stays with your account.
  • If a payment is refunded or charged back, the item it bought is removed.
  • Game portal versions have no purchases.

Accounts

Guest accounts live in your browser. Clearing browser storage without a linked sign-in loses the account. You can delete your account at any time from Profile.

Changes and availability

The game is provided as is. Features, balance, maps and items may change, and seasons reset leaderboards. We aim to keep the game running but cannot promise it will always be available.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/tanayvasishtha/Bowdle. See also the privacy notice.
